Privacy policy

Effective 16 October 2026. This policy covers the Google Calendar connection of Jiaozi, a personal desktop app used only by its owner.

What Jiaozi accesses

Only after the owner connects their Google account, Jiaozi requests these permissions:

  • calendar.calendarlist.readonly: see the list of the owner's calendars;
  • calendar.events.readonly: read events on the owner's calendars, to show them inside the app;
  • calendar.app.created: create one calendar named "Jiaozi" and add, change and remove events in that calendar only.

How the data is used and stored

  • Calendar data is used only to show the owner's schedule in the app and to publish the owner's planned schedule to the "Jiaozi" calendar.
  • Google data is processed on the owner's own computer. The Google sign-in credentials are kept in the operating system's encrypted storage on that computer.
  • Jiaozi does not send Google data to any server run by the developer, does not sell it, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with anyone. Calendar data read from Google is not passed to the AI assistants that work with Jiaozi.

Jiaozi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

  • In Jiaozi: Settings, Connections, External calendars, then disconnect Google.
  • In your Google account: Third-party apps and services.
  • The "Jiaozi" calendar stays in your Google account until you delete it in Google Calendar.
