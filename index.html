Jiaozi

Jiaozi is a personal desktop app that its owner uses to plan their own work and daily schedule, together with AI coding assistants. It runs on the owner's own computers and is not offered to other people.

Google Calendar connection

The Google Calendar connection is optional. When the owner connects their Google account, Jiaozi can:

  • show the owner's existing Google calendars and events inside the app, read-only;
  • publish the schedule planned in Jiaozi to one dedicated calendar named "Jiaozi" in the owner's Google account, so the schedule also appears on the owner's phone. Jiaozi creates, updates and removes events only in that calendar.
